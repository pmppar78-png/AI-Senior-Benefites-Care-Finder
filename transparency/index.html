Transparency Statement

You deserve to know who runs this site and how it makes money. Short answer: one person runs it, and it may earn money from affiliate links and display ads, never from the government programs it covers.

Updated: November 15, 2026 Policy
Important: This page is for informational purposes only. It does not constitute financial, legal, tax, medical, or insurance advice. Always confirm details with official program representatives and licensed professionals before making decisions. Data may change -- verify current figures with official sources.

Who Runs the Site

Senior Benefits Care Finder is owned and run by Paul Paradis. There is no editorial team and no separate business or advertising team: Paul writes the guides and also handles any ad and affiliate relationships himself. Keeping those apart is a rule he applies to himself, described below.

How the Site Makes Money

  • Affiliate links. Some links to commercial services (for example senior living referral services, home care marketplaces, medical alert companies, estate-planning or drug-price sites) may be affiliate or sponsored links. If you use one, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. They are marked with a disclosure.
  • Display ads. The site may show ads, for example from Google AdSense. Ads are served by the ad network and are not chosen or endorsed by the site.

There are no paid placements or sponsored articles inside the guides. If that ever changes, the content will be labeled as sponsored.

How Commercial Relationships Are Kept Apart

  • No company pays to change what a guide says, to be included, or to be left out.
  • Government and nonprofit resources such as Medicare.gov, SHIP, SSA, the VA and USDA are always shown first for program questions, and they are never paid placements.
  • The AI guide is limited to a fixed list of links; for Medicare plan questions it sends people to Medicare.gov and SHIP, not plan sales sites.
  • Figures come from official sources and published surveys regardless of any commercial relationship.

No Government Funding or Affiliation

The site receives no money from CMS, SSA, the VA, USDA, any state agency or any benefits program, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

What the Site Does Not Do

  • It does not sell insurance, Medicare plans or financial products.
  • It does not enroll anyone in a program or file applications.
  • It does not recommend a specific plan or provider for your situation.

Questions
