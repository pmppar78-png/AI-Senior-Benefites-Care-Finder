How Health and Benefits Content Is Checked

This page explains how health-related and benefits content on Senior Benefits Care Finder is checked. Up front: there is no medical, legal or licensed insurance reviewer.

Updated: November 15, 2026 Policy
Important: This page is for informational purposes only. It does not constitute financial, legal, tax, medical, or insurance advice. Always confirm details with official program representatives and licensed professionals before making decisions. Data may change -- verify current figures with official sources.

No Medical or Licensed Reviewer

Senior Benefits Care Finder is written and maintained by one person, Paul Paradis, who is not a doctor, nurse, pharmacist, lawyer, licensed insurance agent or financial advisor. No outside expert reviews the pages. If a qualified reviewer is ever added, this page will name them and say what they review.

What the Content Covers

The guides explain how benefit programs work: who qualifies, what they pay, how to apply. They describe Medicare coverage rules, not medical treatment. They do not diagnose, recommend treatments or medications, or tell anyone which plan to choose.

How It Is Checked Instead

  • Coverage rules and costs are taken from the agency that runs the program: CMS and Medicare.gov, SSA, the VA, USDA, HHS and state agencies.
  • Care costs come from the CareScout 2025 Cost of Care Survey and are labeled as metro, state or national figures.
  • Each figure is labeled with the year it applies to and linked to its source where possible.
  • AI tools help draft and compare pages against the sourced data; Paul reviews the sources and changes himself.

The details are on How We Research and Fact-Checking.

How to Use This Information Safely

  • For a medical question, talk to your doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency call 911.
  • For Medicare plan choices, use the Medicare Plan Finder and free counseling from your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP).
  • For eligibility, confirm with the agency that runs the program; figures here can be out of date or not fit your situation.
  • For legal questions such as Medicaid planning or guardianship, talk to an elder law attorney.

Corrections

If an error is reported or found, it is checked against the primary source, corrected, and, if significant, listed in the corrections log. Report errors to contact@seniorbenefitscarefinder.com.
