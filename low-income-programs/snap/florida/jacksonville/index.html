SNAP in Jacksonville: DCF's Duval County Service Center

Florida's SNAP program is state-run, but Jacksonville residents can still use a local Department of Children and Families Family Resource Center. DCF's directory lists one Duval County site, the Jacksonville Service Center.

DCF Family Resource Center listings and SNAP guidance were reviewed November 15, 2026.

Jacksonville Service Center

Jacksonville Service Center sits at 4080 Woodcock Drive, Jacksonville, FL 32207, according to the DCF center directory. Fax numbers shown for the site are 866-619-5720 and (904) 723-2079. For phone help with an online application, DCF lists its Customer Contact Center at 850-300-4323.

Without an appointment, visitors can drop off documents, authenticate ID, use a computer to apply for government assistance or receive department forms. Anything beyond those tasks needs a booked visit. The appointment portal is the place to schedule that kind of visit.

Former SUNCAP households in Duval

Jacksonville SSI recipients who received SUNCAP should read DCF's transition notice and continue through MyACCESS if instructed. The Duval service center can be useful for identity authentication or document drop-off, but it is not a place for unscheduled full casework.

The Florida SNAP guide covers DCF's statewide program changes and benefit calculation.

A Jacksonville conversation before applying

ElderSource's HelpLine serves Duval and six neighboring counties as an Aging and Disability Resource Center. Specialists discuss the caller's needs, explain available resources, assist with enrollment in eligible programs and make referrals. Call (904) 391-6699 or (888) 242-4464 on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Its Jacksonville office welcomes walk-ins at 10688 Old St. Augustine Road, Jacksonville, FL 32257. The HelpLine can arrange language interpretation and takes after-hours voicemail. An older resident unable to prepare meals can also ask about delivered meals or senior-center meal sites rather than assuming an EBT card alone resolves the problem.

Keep the two agencies' roles separate: ElderSource helps navigate local support, while the DCF service center receives state-benefit paperwork. When following up, have the name of the organization and the type of assistance requested handy so a referral is not mistaken for a filed SNAP application.

City senior centers that serve meals

Jacksonville's Senior Services Division runs 15 community and senior centers for residents 60 and over. Every weekday those centers serve a hot midday meal that meets at least one-third of the daily Recommended Dietary Allowance, and breakfast is offered at 14 sites. The division also lists limited grocery-shopping assistance through the centers, counseling, and information and referral.

For meal times or a center near home, call the nearest center or (904) 255-5400. A senior center lunch and a SNAP card work together: one covers a weekday meal, the other the groceries for evenings and weekends.

Frequently asked questions

Can I walk into the Jacksonville DCF service center?

Yes, for listed tasks: dropping off documents, authenticating ID, using a computer to apply and receiving forms. Other services are by appointment.

How many Duval Family Resource Centers does DCF list?

Just one: the Woodcock Drive office.

Do Jacksonville senior centers serve free meals?

The city says 15 centers serve hot weekday midday meals to residents 60 and over; call (904) 255-5400 for details.
