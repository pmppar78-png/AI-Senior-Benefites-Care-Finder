Tampa SNAP Help Through DCF

In Hillsborough County, DCF's directory currently points Tampa residents to one Family Resource Center. For older applicants who need more personal help, DCF's community partner network can supplement that office without making eligibility decisions.

DCF directory and community partner information opened November 15, 2026.

Tampa Service Center

The DCF directory lists Tampa Service Center at 9393 North Florida Avenue, Tampa, FL 33612, with fax 888-254-7591. It is the only Hillsborough entry in the current list.

Walk-in services are limited to document drop-off, ID authentication, computer use for applications and forms. Book other assistance through DCF's appointment portal.

Community help in Hillsborough

DCF's Community Partner Network includes self-serve sites with computers and staffed partners who can help submit an application, renew benefits, use MyACCESS and check application status.

The Florida senior SNAP guide covers the SUNCAP transition and benefit rules.

Hillsborough's Elder Helpline

Senior Connection Center is the Area Agency on Aging serving Hillsborough, Polk, Manatee, Highlands and Hardee counties. Its Elder Helpline is 1-800-963-5337, and the organization lists 8928 Brittany Way, Tampa, FL 33619 as its contact address. Specialists can connect callers with nutrition and meal programs, benefits assistance, transportation and caregiver support.

The center recommends calling because a conversation lets a specialist sort through concerns and identify the type of help needed. It also offers an online community referral request for people who cannot call or prefer to write. Someone asking for a parent can describe practical difficulties, such as getting groceries home or managing paperwork, rather than naming only one benefit.

For relatives outside Florida, the same page points to the nationwide Eldercare Locator. Save the MyACCESS confirmation separately from any referral paperwork.

Hillsborough County senior meal services

Hillsborough County's Aging Services offers a hot noon weekday lunch at nutrition centers around the county and home-delivered meals for people 60 and older who cannot leave home because of illness, disability or frailty. The county charges no fee and accepts donations. A waiting list ranks applicants by need.

To apply, call the Aging Services Customer Care Team at (813) 272-5250. The listed office is 601 E. Kennedy Blvd., Tampa, FL 33602. A person on the meal waiting list can apply for SNAP through MyACCESS in the meantime.

Renewing a Tampa case

Florida renewals are filed through MyACCESS. A staffed DCF community partner can help with the renewal and check its status, which suits a resident who has no computer at home. Keep a copy of the confirmation page: it is the proof that the renewal reached DCF on time.

Worked example: a Tampa pension under the 200% line

A single retiree in Tampa with $2,500 a month from a pension and Social Security is under Florida's 200% gross line, $2,660 for one person in DCF's Appendix A-1, and USDA's chart lists no Florida asset limit under BBCE. Frequently asked questions

Where is the Hillsborough DCF Family Resource Center?

DCF lists Tampa Service Center at 9393 North Florida Avenue, Tampa, FL 33612.

How do Tampa seniors sign up for county meals?

Through the county's Aging Services phone team, (813) 272-5250. Meals are free, with donations accepted, and there is a waiting list based on need.
