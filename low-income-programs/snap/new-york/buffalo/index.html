Buffalo and Erie County: SNAP for Older Adults

In Buffalo, SNAP cases are handled by the Erie County Department of Social Services, whose main offices are in the Rath Building downtown. The county lets older applicants apply online, by mail, by fax or in person, and the Department for the Aging adds a home-meal program.

Sources: the Erie County Department of Social Services and its Department for the Aging, both consulted Nov. 15, 2026.

Ways to apply in Erie County

The Erie County SNAP page lists four routes. Online, the county directs applicants to New York's myBenefits site. A printed application can be faxed to (716) 858-6834 or mailed to Erie County Department of Social Services, P.O. Box 120, Buffalo, NY 14201, Attention: SNAP Division. The county SNAP Call Center is (716) 858-7239.

Dropping off documents downtown

Effective May 18, 2026, the county moved its document drop box to the first-floor entrance of the Rath Building at 95 Franklin St. It was previously in the vestibule at 158 Pearl St. Use the new location for verification papers so they are not delayed.

Benefit ceilings posted by the county

Erie County's benefits page lists maximum monthly allotments as of October 1, 2026: $306 for one person, $562 for two, $808 for three and $1,023 for four. It stresses that a full SNAP budget has to be calculated, so most older households receive less than the maximum.

Home-delivered meals from the Department for the Aging

Calling (716) 858-8526 starts an in-home evaluation for home-delivered meals and other services. People 60 and over who cannot shop or cook receive two meals around midday, Monday to Friday, planned by registered dietitians; a doctor's note specifying the diet is required. The suggested confidential contribution is $3.50 a meal ($7 a day), and no one is refused for being unable to pay.

FeedMore WNY, (716) 822-2002, delivers in Buffalo and many southern and eastern towns; Amherst and Ken-Ton have their own Meals on Wheels programs.

Renewing without another trip to the Rath Building

New York myBenefits offers more than the initial application: its menu includes Recertify Benefits, Report Changes, View Case Details and Submit Verification Documents. A Buffalo resident who already has a case can use those options to send a pension update or renewal proof instead of making another downtown visit. The portal also has EBT balance and PIN tools. Keep the confirmation from an online submission with the notice that requested it; the date on a renewal packet still matters even when nothing in the household has changed.

Need Help?

The SNAP Call Center at (716) 858-7239 answers case questions, and (716) 858-8526 starts a home-meal assessment. The New York page explains how a retiree's resources are reviewed.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Erie County DSS drop box?

Since May 18, 2026 it has been at the first-floor entrance of the Rath Building, 95 Franklin St., Buffalo.

What is the Erie County SNAP fax number?

Applications can be faxed to (716) 858-6834.

Do Erie County home meals cost money?

A $3.50 contribution per meal is suggested, but nobody is denied meals for not paying.
