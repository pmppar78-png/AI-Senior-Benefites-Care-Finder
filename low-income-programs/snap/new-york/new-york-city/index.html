SNAP for Older New Yorkers in the Five Boroughs

The benefit is SNAP everywhere in New York State, but inside New York City the agency that takes the application, interviews the household and issues the decision is the Human Resources Administration (HRA), part of the city's Department of Social Services. Older residents deal with HRA, not a county office.

HRA, ACCESS NYC, ACCESS HRA and NYC Aging pages were read November 15, 2026.

Applying online with ACCESS HRA

ACCESS HRA handles SNAP applications, recertifications, periodic reports and case changes. According to ACCESS NYC, the Cash Assistance application on ACCESS HRA covers Cash Assistance, SNAP and Medicaid at once, so someone who has already applied for cash help does not need a separate SNAP application.

After the application comes an interview and a request for documents. The city gives a decision within 30 days and mails the EBT card within five business days of approval. Photo ID, utility bills and a lease are among the documents most often requested.

Paper applications and the mail unit

A printed application can be picked up at a SNAP center or requested from the HRA Infoline at 718-557-1399. Completed forms and copies of documents go to: Division of SNAP Services, Mail Application & Referral Unit (MARU), P.O. Box 29008, Brooklyn, NY 11202. Calling 311 and asking for SNAP information also works.

When the only reference number is an application confirmation number, the NYC HRA Document Upload app accepts requested documents without a case number or login.

HRA SNAP centers by borough

HRA's location list shows these sites, all open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.:

  • Bronx: Crotona, 1910 Monterey Ave., 5th Fl.; Hunts Point, 845 Barretto St., B-Level.
  • Brooklyn: Coney Island, 1912 Mermaid Ave.; East New York, 2400 Fulton St.; Williamsburg, 2 George St.
  • Manhattan: East End, 2322 Third Ave.; Washington Heights, 4055 10th Ave.; SNAP services also at the Lower Manhattan (109 E. 16th St.) and St. Nicholas (132 W. 125th St.) Benefits Access Centers.
  • Queens: Jamaica, 165-08 88th Ave.; Queens, 32-20 Northern Blvd., Long Island City; Rockaway, 219 Beach 59th St.
  • Staten Island: Richmond, 201 Bay St., 2nd Fl.

Meals and help at home through NYC Aging

The city's meals for older adults page points residents who can travel to lunch at an Older Adult Center, and residents 60 and older who have difficulty getting out to home-delivered meals. Both can be arranged through 311.

Home-delivered meals are coordinated through NYC Aging's in-home services. A contracted case-management agency does a phone intake and a home visit, and the case manager can also help the person apply for benefits and entitlements. Aging Connect is 212-AGING-NYC (212-244-6469).

Need Help?

HRA's Infoline (718-557-1399) and 311 handle SNAP questions; Aging Connect, 212-244-6469, arranges home meals. Rules that apply across the state, including ESAP, are on the New York page.

Frequently asked questions

Who runs SNAP in New York City?

HRA, the Human Resources Administration, administers SNAP in all five boroughs under state supervision.

Where do I mail a paper SNAP application in NYC?

To the Division of SNAP Services, MARU, P.O. Box 29008, Brooklyn, NY 11202.

Can I upload documents without a case number?

Yes. The NYC HRA Document Upload app works with the application confirmation number alone.
