Yonkers SNAP Help for Residents 60 and Older

Yonkers is in Westchester County, so SNAP applications are handled by the Westchester County Department of Social Services. The county runs a district office in Yonkers and a countywide nutrition program for adults 60 and older.

Westchester County DSS and Senior Programs and Services pages were read November 15, 2026.

The Yonkers District Office

The Westchester DSS office list gives the Yonkers District Office at 131 Warburton Avenue, Yonkers, NY 10701, open 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. on weekdays except holidays. Customer Service for all four district offices is (914) 995-3333; the same number will mail an application.

Online applications go through New York's myBenefits portal, which the county links from its SNAP page. DSS then contacts the applicant to schedule the required eligibility interview.

What Westchester asks for

The county lists proof of identity, age, Social Security numbers and citizenship or immigration status for everyone in the home, plus proof of earned and unearned income and of the address. A specific line applies to older households: Assets (Age 60+ Only), meaning proof of bank accounts or other resources when anyone in the home is over 60.

Housing costs, medical bills for a member over 60 or disabled, and dependent-care costs are optional to provide, but the county notes that sharing them can raise the benefit.

Senior meals in Yonkers

Westchester's nutrition program serves adults 60 and older at dining centers and by home delivery to medically eligible homebound seniors, with pre-registration required. Contributions are voluntary and income is not a condition. Yonkers sites in the county directory include the Yonkers Office for the Aging and Peter Chema Senior Nutrition Center, 435 Riverdale Ave. ((914) 963-6662), and the Scotti Center, 680 Bronx River Road ((914) 377-6490).

Other listed Yonkers locations are the Nepperhan Community Center, 342 Warburton Ave., and North Yonkers Preservation and Development, 219 Ridge Ave.

Renewal packets and card service

Westchester says it mails a recertification packet about two months before expiration. Return the forms and any requested proof rather than waiting for the last benefit deposit. The county also publishes a separate EBT service number, (888) 328-6399, available around the clock in eight languages. Use it for a damaged or missing card and balance questions, not to ask whether DSS approved a medical deduction. Online renewal and verification uploads are available on myBenefits, while the Yonkers office handles the eligibility review.

Frequently asked questions

Where do Yonkers residents apply for SNAP in person?

At the Westchester DSS Yonkers District Office, 131 Warburton Ave., weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Will Westchester ask about my savings?

Yes, the county asks for proof of bank accounts or resources when anyone in the home is over 60.

Is there an interview for a Westchester SNAP case?

Yes. After an application is filed, Westchester DSS reaches out to set one up, and it is required.
