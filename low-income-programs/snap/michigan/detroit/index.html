Detroit Seniors and Michigan Food Assistance

Detroit food assistance cases belong to the Wayne County offices of the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services. The city has several MDHHS district and service centers, and the Detroit Area Agency on Aging runs Meals on Wheels and information services for older residents.

MDHHS Wayne County directory pages, the Restaurant Meal Program page and DAAA contacts were read November 15, 2026.

Finding the right Wayne County office

The MDHHS Wayne County page lists offices by area, including the Conner, Grandmont and Southwest service centers and the Gratiot/Seven Mile, Greenfield/Joy and Greydale/Grand River districts, and it has a ZIP code lookup that assigns each address to one office. Use the lookup rather than visiting the nearest building.

Wayne County Central Administration is at 3040 W. Grand Blvd., Suite 5-650, Detroit, MI 48202; the phone listed is 844-464-3447.

Restaurant meals on the Bridge Card

Detroit seniors in a FAP group where every member is 60 or older, disabled, homeless or an eligible member's spouse can use the Bridge Card at restaurants in the Restaurant Meal Program. There is nothing to apply for; check the MDHHS restaurant map for Detroit locations before ordering.

Detroit Area Agency on Aging

The Detroit Area Agency on Aging is at 1333 Brewery Park, Suite 200, Detroit, MI 48207. Its information and assistance line is (313) 446-4444, staffed 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.; after hours, an online form gets a reply within 72 hours. DAAA takes referrals for its Meals on Wheels program and schedules non-emergency medical transportation with seven days' notice.

The transport service can cover trips for dialysis, radiology and pharmacy visits, which are also costs that can count toward the SNAP medical deduction if the household pays for them.

Applying

Apply and report changes on MI Bridges. If a document request arrives, upload it there or deliver it to the office the ZIP lookup assigns.

Keeping a Detroit case current

The MDHHS food assistance page says MI Bridges is used to manage the account and report changes in employment, income, family circumstances or address. That address check is especially useful while the state distributes new Bridge Cards. DAAA can help locate services, but a meal referral does not update an MDHHS case. When a renewal asks for evidence, keep the submission receipt and contact the assigned district if the notice still shows missing items; the central administration address alone does not identify the office responsible for every Detroit ZIP code.

Need Help?

DAAA's information line, (313) 446-4444, helps with meals and benefits; MDHHS decides the FAP case. Michigan's asset and restaurant rules are summarized in the state guide.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my MDHHS office in Detroit?

Use the ZIP code lookup on the MDHHS Wayne County page; it assigns each address to a district or service center.

Is restaurant food allowed on a Detroit senior's Bridge Card?

Yes, at restaurants in Michigan's Restaurant Meal Program, provided every member of the FAP group meets the program's criteria.

What does DAAA offer besides meals?

Information and assistance, Meals on Wheels referrals and scheduled non-emergency medical rides.
