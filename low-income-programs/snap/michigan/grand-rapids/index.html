Grand Rapids: Food Assistance for Older Adults

Grand Rapids residents apply for Michigan food assistance through the Kent County MDHHS office downtown. For meals, counseling and in-home help, the Area Agency on Aging of Western Michigan covers Kent County and eight neighboring counties.

Grand Rapids information as of November 15, 2026, drawn from the Kent County MDHHS office directory and AAAWM's nutrition-service listings.

Kent County MDHHS office

The MDHHS directory lists Kent County at 121 Martin Luther King Jr. Street SE, Suite 200, Grand Rapids, MI 49507, with an information line of 616-248-1000. Applications and change reports go through MI Bridges, but the county office handles interviews and questions about a pending case.

A Kent County resident whose only income is Supplemental Security Income may be handled through MiCAP, the Michigan Combined Application Project that MDHHS lists for SSI recipients. Ask the office whether that route applies before completing a full application.

Area Agency on Aging of Western Michigan

AAAWM, at 3215 Eaglecrest Dr. NE, Grand Rapids, MI 49525, serves Allegan, Ionia, Kent, Lake, Mason, Mecosta, Montcalm, Newaygo and Osceola counties. Call (616) 456-5664 for help finding resources, Medicare and Medicaid help, caregiver support and in-home services.

Meals on Wheels Western Michigan

Meals on Wheels Western Michigan, 2900 Wilson Ave. SW, Grandville, provides meals at various sites and delivers to homebound people; its number is (616) 459-3111. A homebound senior can receive these meals and still use food assistance for groceries.

For a hot meal out, Michigan's Restaurant Meal Program lets eligible senior households use the Bridge Card at participating restaurants, listed on the MDHHS map.

MI Bridges for renewals and proof

Start a food-assistance application on MI Bridges, and return to the account for status, case management and the renewal steps in the notice. Upload the requested verification rather than sending medical records to a general aging-services email address. A rent statement and evidence of insurance premiums serve different deductions, so organize them separately for the worker.

Most Michigan FAP groups have not faced an asset limit since March 1, 2024. MDHHS does retain one for certain disqualified groups, so disclose what the form requests even if savings are normally excluded. At shopping time, prepared restaurant meals require a participating Restaurant Meal Program business; the AAAWM meal-provider listing is a different service, not a list of places where every Bridge Card can buy lunch.

Need Help?

The Kent County office, 616-248-1000, handles pending cases; AAAWM, (616) 456-5664, connects older adults to meals and services. Michigan's FAP overview lists who qualifies for restaurant meals.
