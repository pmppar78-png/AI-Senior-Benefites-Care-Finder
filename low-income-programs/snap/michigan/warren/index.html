Food Assistance for Warren Residents 60 and Older

Warren is in Macomb County, and MDHHS operates a district office in Warren. Macomb County's Office of Senior Services runs Meals on Wheels and a lunch program for those who can get out.

MDHHS Macomb County directory and Macomb County Meals on Wheels pages were read November 15, 2026.

Macomb - Warren District office

The MDHHS Macomb directory lists the Warren District at 13041 E. 10 Mile Road, Warren, MI 48089, phone 586-427-0600, fax 586-427-0668. The county's main office is at 44777 N. Gratiot Ave., Suite A, Clinton Township (586-412-6100).

Macomb County Meals on Wheels

Meals on Wheels brings a ready-to-eat meal Monday through Friday to Macomb residents 60 or older who are homebound and unable to prepare full meals, regardless of income. The suggested contribution is $3.50 a meal. Some participants can also get a chilled dinner delivered at the same time, and holiday meals are offered for Easter, Thanksgiving and Christmas.

To apply, call the Office of Senior Services at 586-469-5228; staff complete the application by phone. People who do not qualify can buy the meals at a full cost of $8, and a senior who can travel may use a Dining Senior Style site for lunch.

Applying and using the card

File through MI Bridges and watch for the new chip- and tap-enabled Bridge Cards MDHHS says are coming later in 2026. Eligible senior households can also use the Restaurant Meal Program at participating restaurants.

Sending proof and watching renewal dates

MI Bridges lets Warren applicants manage food assistance and report changes without a trip to 10 Mile Road. Use the renewal instructions and due date in the case notice; meal delivery registration with Macomb Senior Services is not a SNAP renewal. Paper verification can go by the Warren district fax listed above, and a copy of the transmission report is useful if receipt is questioned.

For medical deductions, include recurring insurance premiums as well as individual pharmacy receipts. MDHHS specifically names dental care, medical supplies and insurance in its eligible expense examples. A paid-off home can mean a smaller shelter deduction, making those medical costs particularly important to document.

Need Help?

The Warren District office, 586-427-0600, handles local FAP cases; Macomb Senior Services, 586-469-5228, takes meal applications. Read the Michigan overview for the asset rule.

Frequently asked questions

Which MDHHS office serves Warren residents?

The Macomb - Warren District is at 13041 E. 10 Mile Road, Warren, MI 48089; call 586-427-0600.

Is Macomb Meals on Wheels free?

A $3.50 donation per meal is suggested; eligibility does not depend on income.

Does a paid-off home count against food assistance in Michigan?

No. The home is excluded, and most Michigan FAP groups have no asset limit at all.
