San Diego CalFresh: County Help for Older Adults

The County of San Diego Health and Human Services Agency administers CalFresh. Its online instructions spell out several application routes, so an older resident can choose a phone, paper, online or office route without losing the filing date.

San Diego HHSA CalFresh pages were checked November 15, 2026.

Application routes in San Diego

San Diego's how-to-apply page says BenefitsCal can be used to apply, track an application, upload documents, see notices, submit a SAR 7 and recertify. The county's Access Customer Service Call Center, 1-866-262-9881, can mail application forms.

Applicants can return the paper form by mail, by fax to 619-236-9167 or at a Family Resource Center. The application date is when a center receives it. The county allows applicants to state a telephone or in-person interview preference.

A downtown office that handles CalFresh

The Central Region Family Resource Center, Centre City, is at 1255 Imperial Avenue, 5th Floor, San Diego, CA 92101, open Monday through Friday from 7 a.m. to 5 p.m. It makes eligibility determinations for CalFresh, Medi-Cal and General Relief, and its listed phone is the county access line, 1-866-262-9881.

Family Resource Centers and fast help

HHSA's Family Resource Center page warns that not every location provides every program. Choose a center that offers CalFresh before traveling. Proof of identity is needed for the expedited evaluation that can provide benefits within three days.

For eligibility and deduction rules, read the CalFresh guide for older Californians.

San Diego's document list is flexible

The county's documents page lists several ways to establish identity, including a driver's license, work or school ID, voter registration card or birth certificate. For other income, it suggests an award letter or a current benefit check, such as Social Security or disability.

It lists medical evidence separately: insurance records, doctor or hospital bills, drug receipts and pharmacy statements. Housing and utility costs can be verified through a statement on the application, a periodic report, recertification or a verbal statement. Do not delay applying solely because you are unsure which record is best; discuss the county's alternatives.

San Diego also says 2-1-1 can help complete an application, send a packet, connect a caller with a community-based organization or identify the nearest Family Resource Center. That can be a useful first call when a resident knows what help is needed but cannot work through BenefitsCal independently.

Meals from Aging & Independence Services

San Diego County's Aging & Independence Services contracts with nutrition centers that serve weekday lunches to adults 60 and older. Home-delivered meals start with a home visit to assess need. If approved, a hot meal arrives each weekday with frozen meals for the weekend, and eligibility is reviewed quarterly. Each meal is a voluntary donation. Not every city is covered, so check the provider list or call the AIS call center at 800-339-4661.

The Restaurant Meals Program in San Diego

The county's Restaurant Meals Program page requires every member of the CalFresh household to be 60 or older, disabled, homeless or the spouse of someone in those groups. A couple where only one spouse is 60 can still qualify through the spouse rule. Look for the RMP sign at participating restaurants.

Frequently asked questions

What is San Diego CalFresh's application date for paper forms?

The county says it is the date the Family Resource Center receives the application.

Can I ask for a phone interview in San Diego?

Yes. HHSA records a phone or in-person preference along with the application.

Who can use CalFresh at restaurants in San Diego?

The county says every household member must be 60 or older, disabled, homeless or the spouse of such a person.
