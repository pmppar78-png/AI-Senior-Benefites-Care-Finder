CalFresh in Los Angeles for Older Adults

Los Angeles County's Department of Public Social Services runs CalFresh locally. DPSS emphasizes that people receiving SSI/SSP can qualify, a significant point for older residents who once heard that SSI excluded them.

Every DPSS and CDSS page cited here was opened on November 15, 2026.

DPSS application details

LA County DPSS says SSI/SSP recipients became eligible for CalFresh effective June 1, 2019, if they meet other requirements. A household can apply online, by telephone at (866) 613-3777 or in person at a DPSS district office. On that line, option 2 and then option 2 again reaches CalFresh applications; paper applications can also be faxed to (310) 215-8220.

DPSS lists the minimum information to file as name, household address and a signature. A homeless household may use a district address. Expedited CalFresh can be available within three days, and sometimes the same day, for qualifying households.

A downtown-area DPSS district office

The DPSS office locator lists Metro Family, District 13, at 2707 S. Grand Ave., Suite A, Los Angeles, CA 90007, offering CalFresh, CalWORKs and Medi-Cal. Its office profile gives weekday public hours of 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., a participant help line of (626) 569-1399 and fax (213) 743-1480. Residents in other parts of the city can use the same locator to find a closer district office.

Los Angeles options beyond ordinary CalFresh

DPSS notes that homeless, elderly or disabled people may buy prepared meals at participating restaurants. People who are not federally eligible solely because of immigration status may be considered for the state-funded California Food Assistance Program.

The California guide explains statewide income tests and simplified application rules.

When food is needed before the usual interview

DPSS describes expedited service for a household whose monthly gross income and cash resources together are below rent or mortgage and utilities, or whose gross monthly income is less than $150 and cash is $100 or less. It also has a route for certain destitute migrant or seasonal farmworker households. Tell the worker what is happening now rather than waiting for a standard appointment.

An interview and identity verification are still necessary, but DPSS says photo identification is not required. It can try to confirm identity using someone the applicant knows or other readily available records. Approved expedited recipients can have the EBT card mailed or pick it up at a district office.

If you are assigned a later telephone slot, the county page says you can call before the scheduled interview. Keep the application date and the notice in front of you when explaining that the household needs an expedited evaluation.

Restaurant meals and senior dining in LA County

California's Restaurant Meals Program lets CalFresh recipients who are 60 or older, disabled or homeless, along with their spouses, buy prepared meals at approved restaurants. CDSS says the program became statewide on September 1, 2021, and that a recipient can use any participating restaurant in California, even outside their home county. The CDSS page links a county list and a map.

Los Angeles County's Elderly Nutrition Program is separate from CalFresh. To enroll, call 1 (800) 510-2020 and complete the intake. Group meals are open to people 60 and older and their spouses, and home-delivered meals, typically five days a week, go to people who are frail, homebound or isolated. Contributions are voluntary.

ESAP in Los Angeles County

For a household where nobody works and everyone is older or disabled, California's Elderly Simplified Application Project means a 36-month certification and no renewal interview. That project now runs until September 30, 2031 under CDSS All County Letter 26-58 of August 21, 2026. Right now people 60 and over qualify; on March 1, 2027 the age floor rises to 65 for anyone without a disability. When read on November 15, 2026, DPSS's own ESAP policy page still showed the old September 30, 2026 end date.

Frequently asked questions

Can SSI recipients in Los Angeles get CalFresh?

Yes. Receiving SSI/SSP no longer bars CalFresh in Los Angeles County; since June 1, 2019, those recipients can qualify when the other rules are met.

How quickly can Los Angeles CalFresh arrive?

If the household qualifies for expedited service, DPSS aims for benefits within three days, and same-day issuance is possible.

Can LA County seniors use CalFresh at restaurants?

Yes, at approved Restaurant Meals Program vendors, if every member is 60 or older, disabled, homeless or the spouse of such a person.
