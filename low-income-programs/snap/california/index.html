CalFresh for Older Californians

In California, SNAP is called CalFresh. It is supervised by the California Department of Social Services but operated by counties, which means the practical application and interview arrangements depend on where you live. The benefit is paid through an EBT card and can supplement retirement income; being on SSI does not automatically put a Californian outside the program.

California CDSS and county guidance read November 15, 2026. CDSS's 2026–2027 income insert covers October 1, 2026 through September 30, 2027.

The 200% rule and the older-household alternative

California's entry on USDA's November 12, 2026 BBCE chart reads 200% gross income, with no limit on assets. California materials also call this modified categorical eligibility, or MCE. A qualifying pamphlet provides the non-cash benefit behind the policy.

The CDSS FY2027 income insert gives the monthly gross and net amounts below. The gross line is an initial guide, not a reason to deny an older applicant the separate protections in regular federal SNAP. USDA says a household with an elderly or disabled member outside categorical eligibility may qualify without passing a gross test, using the net-income and applicable resource tests instead. The regular FY2027 resource ceiling for that household is $4,750; specified federal exclusions and categorical exceptions may remove some or all resources from consideration.

A county worker should explain how MCE and the elderly rules apply to the actual case. Do not treat a web calculator as the final word if it fails to ask about medical bills or disability.

What counts as a CalFresh household?

The food arrangement matters more than whether people are related or share a lease. Adults who regularly buy food and prepare meals together are generally one group, while roommates who truly keep food separate may be different groups. Federal rules keep spouses living together in the same household and normally include a child under 22 living with a parent.

A person at least 60 who cannot purchase and prepare food because of a permanent disability may, with a spouse, qualify as a separate household under a special rule tied to the income of the other residents. Describe the arrangement at the interview rather than simply selecting every name on a rental agreement. The county must evaluate the exception.

Deductible costs and the benefit amount

For FY2027, CDSS lists maximum CalFresh allotments of $306 for one person, $562 for two and $808 for three. Those maxima are not automatic payments to every eligible senior: net income usually reduces them by roughly 30%, with federal rounding rules. The federal minimum for an eligible household of one or two people is $25.

The standard deduction is $217 for one to three members. An elderly or disabled member can also claim eligible medical costs above $35 per month that are not reimbursed by insurance or another person. Think beyond prescriptions: verified insurance premiums, dental costs, dentures and qualifying travel for care can matter. A special diet itself is not deductible.

Eligible housing and utilities can produce an excess-shelter deduction after the other adjustments. Older or disabled households are not bound by the ordinary shelter cap. Provide the county with the current rent or mortgage amount and requested expense records; high costs cannot affect the calculation if the worker never receives them.

Applying through the county and BenefitsCal

BenefitsCal is the online benefits channel. County offices also accept applications outside the website. Los Angeles County DPSS, for example, explains that a name, address or acceptable alternative for someone without housing, and a signature can start filing; the interview and remaining evidence follow. An applicant should not wait for a perfect packet before asking for help.

Local guides cover Los Angeles, San Diego and San Jose. When urgent food needs arise, ask about expedited CalFresh. Los Angeles and San Diego describe benefits within three days for qualifying emergencies, including some households whose income and cash cannot cover housing costs.

Ask about the Elderly Simplified Application Project rather than assuming every retired household qualifies. CDSS All County Letter 26-58 (August 21, 2026) extends ESAP through September 30, 2031. Until February 28, 2027 it covers households in which everyone is 60 or older or disabled with no earned income; from March 1, 2027, members who are not disabled must be 65 or older. Never disregard a reporting or interview notice on the strength of a general ESAP description.

Restaurant meals are a limited exception

California's Restaurant Meals Program lets eligible CalFresh households use food benefits at approved restaurant vendors, not at every restaurant that takes an EBT cash card. CDSS says every household member must fit an eligible category: age 60 or older, disability, homelessness, or being the spouse of someone in an eligible group.

The state vendor map and list identify participating businesses. Eligible recipients may use an approved vendor outside their home county, but a household containing a member who does not meet RMP rules cannot rely on this option. For everyone else, the usual hot-prepared-food exclusions still apply.

If a county denies or reduces CalFresh and the reasoning seems wrong, keep the notice and request the hearing it describes. USDA's SNAP guidance gives 90 days from the disputed decision to request a fair hearing. A request for an explanation and a formal hearing request are not necessarily the same thing, so be clear about what you are asking for.

Frequently asked questions

Is California CalFresh a different benefit from SNAP?

CalFresh is California's name for the federal SNAP food benefit. CDSS supervises it, counties operate it, and the approved amount goes onto an EBT card.

Can every CalFresh senior buy a hot restaurant meal?

Only a household that meets Restaurant Meals Program criteria can use that exception, and only at an approved participating vendor. Age 60 for one resident does not make a mixed household automatically eligible.
