CalFresh Support for Older Adults in San Jose

San Jose residents apply for CalFresh through the County of Santa Clara. For older adults wanting personal help, Sourcewise offers application assistance for county residents in person and by phone.

Santa Clara County handbook pages and Sourcewise information were opened November 15, 2026.

Santa Clara County's benefit offices

The county Social Services Agency's contact directory lists the Benefits Service Center at 1867 Senter Road, San Jose, CA 95112 and Continuing Benefit Services at 1877 Senter Road. The Department of Employment and Benefit Services number for applications or CalFresh questions is (408) 758-3800; published hours are Monday–Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., except county holidays.

For an existing case, the agency says the fastest route is to call the worker directly. That person's contact details are in the top-right corner of the last Notice of Action. If the notice is missing, the department number can help route the question.

The county Social Services Agency contact directory (county contact directory on sccgov.org, checked November 15, 2026) separately lists the North County Office in Mountain View and the South County Office in Gilroy. A San Jose applicant should distinguish those county eligibility locations from Sourcewise, which helps with the application but does not issue the award. Keep the latest Notice of Action for follow-up even if someone else helped submit the form.

Sourcewise application assistance

Sourcewise says its Community Resource Specialists help Santa Clara County residents who meet income eligibility complete CalFresh applications. Appointments average about one hour and are available in person or by telephone at (408) 350-3200, option 1.

Bring income and expense information to the appointment. Sourcewise can also explain what happens after the form is filed.

County rules for elderly households

Santa Clara's ESAP handbook describes elderly or disabled households with no earned income, a 36-month certification and no renewal interview unless an exception applies. CDSS All County Letter 26-58 extends ESAP through September 30, 2031, and from March 1, 2027 members who are not disabled must be 65 or older. It also says the CalFresh ABAWD exemption screening process took effect June 1, 2026, with form CF 377.11E in renewal packets.

The California guide explains statewide CalFresh limits and deductions.

Home-delivered meals in Santa Clara County

The county's Senior Nutrition Program arranges home-delivered meals for residents 60 and older who are frail, homebound or isolated. Enrollment questions go to (408) 755-7680. Sourcewise delivers Trio Community Meals countywide, 12 frozen meals a week plus groceries; call (408) 350-3246 to start, pause or cancel. In San Jose, Loaves and Fishes, (408) 922-9085, delivers seven frozen Asian meals a week, and the Vietnamese American Services Center, (408) 518-6200, delivers within four miles of 2410 Senter Road. Nobody is turned away for not contributing.

CalFresh can also be spent at approved restaurants through California's Restaurant Meals Program by a household whose members are all 60 or older, disabled or homeless, or are spouses of such members.

Frequently asked questions

Who can get Sourcewise help with CalFresh?

Sourcewise serves Santa Clara County residents who meet the income eligibility requirements.

What changed in Santa Clara renewal packets in June 2026?

Renewal packets now carry form CF 377.11E, the ABAWD exemption screening that started June 1, 2026.

How do San Jose seniors get meals delivered?

Call the Santa Clara County Senior Nutrition Program at (408) 755-7680, or Sourcewise at (408) 350-3246 for Trio Community Meals.
