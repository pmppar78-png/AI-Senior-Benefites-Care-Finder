SNAP in Chicago for Residents 60 and Older

Chicago has no city SNAP office of its own: applications go through the state's ABE system and are handled at IDHS Family Community Resource Centers spread across the city. Meals for homebound older adults come from the city's Department of Family and Support Services.

IDHS Cook County office listings and Chicago DFSS pages were read November 15, 2026.

Applying and renewing online

ABE takes the application and, through Manage My Case, shows the redetermination date and lets the household update its mailing address. ABE is offline on Sunday mornings from 6 to 11 a.m., and starting October 30, 2026, a household may hold only one Manage My Case account. A relative who helps with paperwork should use the same account rather than opening a second one.

Home-delivered meals from DFSS

Chicago's Department of Family and Support Services provides home-delivered meals to people 60 and older who are frail, homebound because of illness or disability, or otherwise isolated. The DFSS main line is 312-743-0300, and 311 offers 24-hour assistance; the department is at 1615 W. Chicago Ave., 5th Floor.

Meals do not reduce a SNAP benefit, and a person can use both.

Prepared meals using the Link Card

The IDHS Restaurant Meals search for Cook County returns actual participating locations, not just a program description. Examples are BJ's Market & Bakery, 1737 E. 95th St., and Chef Daddy's, 5944 W. Lake St. The listings describe the service as connecting elderly, disabled and homeless SNAP customers with restaurants where Link can pay for meals. Check the current search before visiting: an ordinary restaurant accepting credit cards does not automatically accept SNAP, and participation does not add money to the Link account.

Need Help?

Call the Family Community Resource Center nearest you or IDHS Help is Here at 1-800-843-6154; DFSS, 312-743-0300, handles senior meals. For the 165% and 200% income lines, read the Illinois overview.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Chicago SNAP office separate from IDHS?

No. Chicago cases are handled by IDHS Family Community Resource Centers, with applications through ABE.

Who delivers meals to homebound seniors in Chicago?

The Department of Family and Support Services runs home-delivered meals for residents 60 and older; call 312-743-0300 or 311.

Is the Englewood resource center open?

IDHS shows Englewood operating out of 1642 W. 59th St. for now; call (773) 918-6700 before going.
