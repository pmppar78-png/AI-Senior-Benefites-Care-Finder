SNAP for Naperville Residents 60 and Over

Naperville sits mainly in DuPage County, and the county's only IDHS Family Community Resource Center is in Naperville itself. Older residents can therefore do their interview nearby and pair SNAP with DuPage meal programs.

Sources: the IDHS office locator for DuPage County and AgeGuide's meal listings, both opened November 15, 2026.

The DuPage County resource center

According to the IDHS DuPage listing, the Family Community Resource Center is at 1717 Park St., Suite 105, Naperville, IL 60563. Call (630) 328-1000 (TTY (866) 322-3092); documents can be faxed to (331) 702-8935. Residents of the Will County part of Naperville should check that county's listing.

Meals through DuPage County

For home-delivered meals, AgeGuide names DuPage County Community Services as the care coordination unit, at (630) 407-6500 or (800) 942-9412. The DuPage Senior Citizens Council, (630) 620-0804, is the meal provider and also runs community dining.

Two Naperville dining sites appear in the AgeGuide list: Golconda Indian Restaurant & Banquets, 1568 W. Ogden Ave., Suite 140, on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. (reservations at 630-642-8513), and Metropolitan Asian Family Services, 1032 E. Ogden Ave. There is no income or asset test for these meals.

Online steps

Apply on ABE, which also shows renewal dates. Only one Manage My Case account per household is allowed from October 30, 2026.

Before a renewal or a community lunch

The county meal network and SNAP have different intake routes. DuPage Community Services assesses the need for delivery; IDHS evaluates the food benefit. AgeGuide says meals require no income or asset qualification, and inability to donate does not prevent participation. A senior turned down for SNAP can therefore still ask about dining or delivery. Call a dining site before traveling, since AgeGuide cautions that service days can change.

The DuPage Restaurant Meals search in the IDHS locator produced no results on the review date. A community meal at an AgeGuide site should not be confused with buying a restaurant meal on Link. For the SNAP case itself, ABE Manage My Case provides the renewal date, and the Park Street center can clarify a notice that asks for more proof.

Need Help?

The DuPage resource center at (630) 328-1000 handles Naperville interviews; DuPage County Community Services, (630) 407-6500, starts meal referrals. The Illinois overview describes household and renewal rules.

Frequently asked questions

Where do Naperville residents go for SNAP help?

The IDHS Family Community Resource Center at 1717 Park St., Suite 105, Naperville.

Who arranges home-delivered meals in DuPage County?

DuPage County Community Services does the assessment; call (630) 407-6500 or (800) 942-9412.

Do property taxes count in a SNAP budget?

Yes, property taxes and homeowner's insurance count as shelter costs along with the mortgage.
