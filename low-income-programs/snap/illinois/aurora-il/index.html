Older Aurora Residents and Illinois SNAP

Aurora crosses county lines, so the first step is confirming which county the IDHS office locator assigns. Kane County residents are served by an IDHS Family Community Resource Center on West Old Indian Trail.

IDHS Kane County listings and AgeGuide nutrition information were read November 15, 2026.

The Aurora resource center

The IDHS Kane County listing shows the Aurora Family Community Resource Center at 361 W. Old Indian Trail, Aurora, IL 60506. Phone (630) 844-7400, TTY (866) 323-0616, fax (630) 844-7499. The county's other center is in Elgin, at 700 S. State St.

Faxing paper verification to the Aurora number can be quicker than mailing, but keep the fax confirmation page.

Senior meals in Kane County

AgeGuide, the area agency on aging for northeastern Illinois, lists Senior Services Associates as the Kane County care coordination unit for home-delivered meals; its Aurora number is (630) 897-4035. A care coordinator does an in-home assessment, and meals arrive at midday up to five days a week.

There is no income or asset test for these meals, and a donation is suggested but not required. For community dining, the Kane County provider is Kane Senior Council, (630) 338-0999.

How Aurora residents apply

Use ABE for the application and Manage My Case for renewals, or call IDHS Help is Here at 1-800-843-6154. The Aurora center handles interviews and in-person questions for Kane County residents.

Renewal and meal choices in Kane County

ABE's Manage My Case displays the redetermination due date; use that date to plan the next submission rather than assuming the Link Card keeps renewing by itself. The one-account-per-household rule means an older applicant and an assisting relative should agree on who maintains the household account. Never share the Link PIN as a substitute for formally naming someone to help.

A Kane County search in the IDHS Restaurant Meals locator returned no locations on November 15, 2026. That is a reason to use the county dining and delivery contacts above for a prepared meal, not to assume every Aurora restaurant can take Link. It also differs from Cook County, where the same search returned participating restaurants.

Need Help?

The Aurora resource center, (630) 844-7400, is the local SNAP office, and Senior Services Associates, (630) 897-4035, handles meal assessments. Income lines for Illinois are explained on the state page.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the IDHS office in Aurora?

At 361 W. Old Indian Trail, Aurora, IL 60506; the phone is (630) 844-7400.

Do Kane County home-delivered meals have an income limit?

AgeGuide says there is no income or asset requirement; a care coordinator assesses need.

What if my Aurora address is in DuPage County?

Use the IDHS locator for your county; DuPage residents are served by the Naperville center.
