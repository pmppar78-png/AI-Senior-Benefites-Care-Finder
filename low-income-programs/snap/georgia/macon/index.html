SNAP Assistance for Older Adults in Macon-Bibb

Macon-Bibb County residents start with Bibb County DFCS, the local Georgia Division of Family & Children Services office. Most case updates, however, can be submitted online, which is helpful for anyone who would rather not wait in an office lobby.

Bibb County DFCS and Georgia SNAP tools were checked against DFCS pages on November 15, 2026.

The Bibb County office

Bibb County DFCS is listed at 456 Oglethorpe St., Macon, GA 31201. The posted schedule is Monday–Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Eastern Time. The Census Bureau's population estimates name the local government Macon-Bibb County.

For general SNAP questions, DFCS uses its statewide Customer Contact Center at (877) 423-4746. Its online contact form covers Georgia Gateway, EBT cards, food stamps and related programs. The Bibb office's own primary line is (478) 751-6051, and the Region 6 virtual fax, 478-599-9023, is reserved for food stamp, Medicaid or TANF verification.

Keeping a Macon case moving

Use Georgia Gateway for a renewal and supporting documents. DFCS also offers Form 508 for SNAP, Medicaid and TANF renewal. A recipient whose food is destroyed in a household misfortune should report the loss to DFCS and complete Form 841 within 10 days.

DFCS supports ConnectEBT, where cardholders can view deposits and lock or unlock their card. See the Georgia guide for older applicants for rules shared by every county.

Middle Georgia's one-call connection

The Middle Georgia Regional Commission Area Agency on Aging is based at 3661 Eisenhower Pkwy, Suite MB102, Macon, Georgia 31206. Its Aging and Disability Resource Connection screens for programs in the region's 11 counties, then helps connect eligible people with services. The page cautions that funding availability can mean a waiting list.

For aging-services questions it gives 1-866-552-4464, option 2. A family arranging care alongside an application can use that connection to explain needs in one conversation rather than calling unrelated programs. The commission says to call 478-751-6466 if no team member has responded to a referral within 48 hours.

Worked example: a Macon couple

A married couple counts as a two-person household, and USDA's FY2027 maximum for two is $562. If their net income after deductions is $900, Georgia subtracts 30%, or $270, and the couple receives $292 a month. Had one spouse's medical bills been missing from the file, net income would be higher and the allotment lower, which is why receipts matter at interview time.

Where Bibb County notices and renewals live

Gateway accounts show notices and accept a renewal, a document upload or a change report. A renewal left half-done is kept only 24 hours, so a caregiver helping a parent should finish it during the same visit. Because Bibb County DFCS is open weekdays only, uploading on a weekend keeps the paperwork moving without waiting for Monday.

Frequently asked questions

What hours does Bibb County DFCS keep?

The office is open weekdays from 8 a.m. until 5 p.m. Eastern.

How long do I have to report lost food for replacement?

Georgia's SNAP page says to report the loss and submit Form 841 within 10 days.

How much SNAP could a Macon couple receive?

It depends on net income. With $900 in net income, USDA's FY2027 formula gives $562 minus $270, or $292 a month.
