Food Assistance in Augusta for Older Adults

For SNAP, Augusta's in-person county office is Richmond County DFCS. Its location page combines walk-in details with contact options specific to food stamp, Medicaid and TANF paperwork.

Richmond County DFCS location information and Census Vintage 2024 data were accessed November 15, 2026.

Richmond County DFCS details

Richmond County DFCS is listed at 520 Fenwick St., Augusta, GA 30901, open Monday–Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Its posted primary telephone number is (706) 721-3000, and its mailing address is PO Box 2277, Augusta, GA 30903.

For food stamp, Medicaid or TANF questions, the page directs callers to the Customer Contact Center at 1-877-423-4746. It lists 1-888-704-9391 only for verification faxes in those programs. Put the case number on each faxed page if you have it.

Choosing an Augusta application route

An Augusta resident can still apply through Georgia Gateway instead of visiting Fenwick Street. Gateway also handles document uploads, so a pension letter or medical bill photograph may be faster than a second office visit. If everyone in the household is 65 or older, Form 298 can instead be mailed to the statewide Georgia Senior SNAP post office box listed on DFCS's Senior SNAP page.

Before choosing between the general application and Senior SNAP, read the Georgia senior SNAP guide. It explains Georgia's age criterion and benefit tests without repeating them on every local page.

Augusta's aging and disability counselors

The CSRA Regional Commission ADRC offers information and options counseling to older adults, people with disabilities, caregivers and veterans at all income levels. Counselors conduct eligibility screenings, assess needs and refer people to community resources. The office lists 3626 Walton Way Ext., Suite 1, Augusta, GA 30909 and counselor line (706) 210-2018.

This is a resource-navigation service, not another DFCS intake office. A household dealing with an illness, a move home from a nursing facility or difficulty living independently can describe that larger situation to a counselor while Richmond County DFCS processes the food benefit.

The DFCS interview and paper renewals

After the application is filed, DFCS calls for a phone interview. The applicant, another adult in the household or an authorized representative can take the call, but that person needs to know the household's situation. An adult child helping from out of town can be named as representative so the interview is not missed.

At renewal, DFCS accepts the form and proof through Gateway or on Form 508, which comes in standard and large print, English and Spanish. Richmond County's verification fax, 1-888-704-9391, is the right destination for a paper copy.

Worked example: an Augusta retiree on $306

For one person, USDA's FY2027 maximum is $306. If deductions bring a retiree's net income to $600, DFCS subtracts 30% of it, $180, leaving a $126 monthly allotment. A rent increase or new prescription can change the net figure, so report it rather than waiting for renewal.

Where is the Richmond County DFCS office?

What is the Richmond County verification fax for?

DFCS says 1-888-704-9391 is only for food stamp, Medicaid or TANF verification.
