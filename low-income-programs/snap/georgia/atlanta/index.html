Atlanta SNAP: Choosing the Right DFCS Office

Atlanta residents use the DFCS county office for the county where they live. For the Fulton portion of the city, Georgia lists two Fulton offices, while residents of DeKalb have a separate county office. Online filing remains statewide.

DFCS location pages and Senior SNAP guidance opened November 15, 2026.

Fulton and DeKalb DFCS locations

DFCS lists Fulton NW County at 1249 Donald Lee Hollowell Parkway, Atlanta, GA 30318 and Fulton South County at 5710 Stonewall Tell Road, College Park, GA 30349. DeKalb County DFCS is listed at 2300 Parklake Dr. NE, Atlanta, GA 30345. The two Fulton pages show Monday–Friday hours from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

The statewide DFCS Customer Contact Center number, (877) 423-4746, handles SNAP questions. Upload verification through Georgia Gateway when possible so it is attached to the case electronically.

Senior SNAP goes to a central address

For a household using Form 298, DFCS publishes a statewide mailing address: Georgia Senior SNAP, P.O. Box 450149, Atlanta, GA 31145-0149, with fax 678-717-5585. That central route differs from taking a general application to a Fulton or DeKalb office. Keep a copy and note the date sent.

Georgia's Senior SNAP page also allows applications online through Gateway or by telephone. Its published online hours are Monday–Friday, 5 a.m. to midnight, excluding holidays. The Georgia SNAP guide explains the age and BBCE rules once for the whole state.

An Atlanta resource for help beyond the form

Empowerline, a service of the Atlanta Regional Commission's aging network, says it can help people access SNAP and participating farmers-market programs. It also connects residents with senior-center meals, delivered meals and food pantries. Empowerline answers at 404-463-3333.

That is useful when groceries are needed before a DFCS application is decided, or when someone can afford food but cannot shop or cook. Explain the immediate problem rather than asking only for a SNAP form. Empowerline also describes transportation resources for older adults and people with disabilities, including public transit, shuttles and personalized rides; ask what is available for the particular trip.

Renewing online without losing your place

A Georgia Gateway account is used to renew benefits, upload a document, report a change and view notices. Gateway saves a renewal up to the last finished screen, but it deletes an unfinished renewal after 24 hours. Set aside time to complete it in one sitting, or return the next morning at the latest. Paper Form 508 is also available in large print from DFCS.

Worked example: the $25 floor

Consider a 72-year-old Atlanta resident living alone whose net income works out to $1,000 a month. Thirty percent of that is $300, and USDA's FY2027 maximum for one person is $306, which would leave $6. USDA sets a $25 monthly minimum for eligible one- and two-person households, so the benefit would be $25 rather than $6.

Frequently asked questions

Which DFCS office serves Atlanta residents?

That depends on which county the address is in. DFCS lists Fulton NW and Fulton South offices, and DeKalb residents use DeKalb County DFCS.

Where does an Atlanta Senior SNAP Form 298 go?

DFCS lists a statewide Georgia Senior SNAP P.O. box and fax number rather than a county-specific destination.

What is the smallest SNAP benefit an Atlanta senior can get?

USDA's FY2027 floor is $25 a month whenever a household of one or two qualifies.
