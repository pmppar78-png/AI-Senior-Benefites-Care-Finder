How Older Fayetteville Residents Get Food Help From Cumberland DSS

Fayetteville sits in Cumberland County, and the Cumberland County Department of Social Services handles every Food and Nutrition Services case for the city. The county keeps the process mostly paper-friendly, with several ways to hand in documents without waiting in line.

Cumberland County's addresses and phone numbers were taken from the county's site and from NCDHHS on November 15, 2026, while USDA's FY2027 tables supply the income and benefit figures.

Reaching Cumberland County DSS

NCDHHS lists the Cumberland County Department of Social Services, based in Fayetteville. Applications can be filed online through ePASS or in person at the DSS building, 1225 Ramsey Street, Fayetteville, NC 28301. Calling 910-323-1540 gets an application mailed or emailed to you.

The county's FNS (Food Stamps) page lists six ways to turn in applications, recertifications and changes: by mail, in the drop boxes outside the DSS building, at the scanning area on the first floor, by fax to 910-677-2885, through the ePASS portal, or by email.

Deadlines that matter

Cumberland County stresses that recertifications and requested documents must be in by the dates shown on the notice. Seniors who rely on the mail may find the outdoor drop boxes the surest way to meet those dates.

USDA's rules give households with someone 60 or older a few advantages at recertification as well as at application: for a household above the BBCE line only the net income test applies, and medical costs over $35 a month are deducted.

Once the card arrives, the county's FNS page is plain about its limits: benefits cannot be used for tobacco, pet food, paper products, soap products or alcoholic beverages.

Fayetteville income guide

Because North Carolina is on USDA's BBCE chart with no asset limit, a Fayetteville senior's savings usually do not matter if gross income is within 200 percent of poverty. Cumberland caseworkers measure that against $2,660 a month for one person and $3,608 for two; the North Carolina FNS guide has the full list of deductions and the simplified program amounts.

Need Help?

Only Cumberland County's Department of Social Services can approve or deny an FNS application. If you want ideas for other help a Fayetteville retiree might qualify for, ask the AI guide.
