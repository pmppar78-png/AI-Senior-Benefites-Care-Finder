Food and Nutrition Services in Winston-Salem for People 60+

Forsyth County's Department of Social Services, part of the county's Health and Human Services, manages Food and Nutrition Services for Winston-Salem. Its page carries a point older residents in their early 60s should read closely: the expanded work requirement reaches age 64.

Forsyth's FNS page and the NCDHHS site were consulted on November 15, 2026, for the details below, which use USDA's FY2027 standards.

Applying with Forsyth County DSS

Forsyth's Food and Nutrition Services page sends applicants to the NCDHHS FNS site to check eligibility and apply online through ePASS. Anything you need to add to a pending application or open case can be emailed to customercare-user@forsyth.cc or given by phone at 336-703-3800. Social Services itself is at 741 North Highland Avenue, Winston-Salem, NC 27101.

The work requirement and people aged 60 to 64

Forsyth County says the 80-hour monthly work requirement now covers recipients aged 18 to 64, as well as veterans, people experiencing homelessness, young adults who were in foster care, and able-bodied adults whose children are 14 or older. Paid work, job training and school attendance all count toward the hours.

That means a 62-year-old living alone without a disability may be asked to meet the rule, while someone 65 or older is not. USDA says households made up only of elderly or disabled members are not subject to work requirements, so a disability determination can matter. Forsyth also offers More Than A Job NC, the renamed employment and training program, to FNS recipients 16 and older.

Forsyth County figures

Savings rarely matter to a Forsyth FNS application, because North Carolina has no asset test for anyone whose gross income is no more than double the poverty guideline. Measured in dollars, that ceiling is $2,660 for a single person and $3,608 for two in FY2027, figures explained in the North Carolina FNS guide, which also covers the simplified program for SSI recipients 65 and over.

The top monthly benefit is $306 for one person and $562 for two.

Frequently asked questions

I am 63. Does the Forsyth County work requirement apply to me?

It can. Forsyth says the requirement covers recipients aged 18 to 64. Ask DSS about exemptions, such as a disability.

How do I send more information for my Forsyth FNS case?

Email it to customercare-user@forsyth.cc or call 336-703-3800, as listed on the county's FNS page.
