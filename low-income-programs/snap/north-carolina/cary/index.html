Food Assistance for Cary Seniors

Cary's town limits cross three counties. Since Food and Nutrition Services is run county by county, the right office for a Cary household depends on which county its address sits in, not on the town name.

County and state program details were checked on November 15, 2026; federal amounts are for FY2027, which began October 1, 2026.

Wake, Chatham or Durham?

The Town of Cary's own taxes and assessments page explains that property in Cary resides in one of three counties: Wake, Chatham and Durham. Most addresses fall in Wake County, whose Health & Human Services department runs FNS. A household on the Chatham side applies through Chatham County's Department of Social Services, and one in the Durham County portion goes to Durham County DSS.

Your property tax bill shows the county. If you rent, the NCDHHS local DSS directory lists every county agency and can help confirm which one serves you.

Applying through Wake County from Cary

For the Wake portion, the Western Health & Human Services Center at 111 James Jackson Ave. in Cary is one of the county's paper drop-off sites, listed on Wake's application page. ePASS is the statewide online portal, so residents of all three counties can use it.

Wake's farmers market page lists the Cary Farmers Market, at 160 E. Park St., among markets that take EBT and Double Bucks, a match program that stretches SNAP dollars on fresh produce. It runs on Saturdays all year: 8 a.m. to noon from April through October, and 9 a.m. to noon from November through March.

Limits that apply in all three counties

Eligibility rules are statewide, so a Cary senior sees the same numbers whichever county handles the case. North Carolina uses broad-based categorical eligibility, which removes the asset test for households under 200 percent of poverty. That ceiling works out to $2,660 a month for someone on their own and $3,608 for two people, figures the statewide FNS guide derives from USDA's FY2027 tables.

The benefit itself is the maximum for the household size, $306 for one person or $562 for two, reduced by 30 percent of net income. The same guide explains the medical and shelter deductions that lower net income.

Frequently asked questions

I live in Cary but my address is in Chatham County. Where do I apply?

Chatham County's Department of Social Services handles your case. You can still apply online through ePASS.

Does the Cary Farmers Market take EBT?

Wake County lists it as accepting EBT and Double Bucks.
