Greensboro and High Point: FNS Help for People Over 60

Guilford County's Department of Social Services works out of two offices, one in Greensboro and one in High Point. Both handle Food and Nutrition Services, and the county has posted unusually specific rules about when renewal paperwork counts as late.

Guilford's office, renewal and energy-help details reflect county and state pages as of November 15, 2026. Benefit amounts follow USDA's FY2027 tables.

Applying in Guilford County

The Guilford County Department of Social Services has two offices on NCDHHS's list: 1203 Maple St., Greensboro, NC 27405, and 325 E Russell Ave., High Point, NC 27260. Its mailing address is P.O. Box 3388, Greensboro, NC 27402, and the main phone is 336-641-3000.

On its economic services page, the county directs applicants to ePASS or a paper form, available in English and Spanish, and says to call 336-641-3000 for more information about food assistance. Questions about an EBT card already in hand go to 1-888-622-7328 instead.

Renewals: the 15th-of-the-month rule

Guilford says renewals happen at least every six or 12 months and that current customers get a notice by mail. Recertifications are due at the beginning of the month they are due, and any received after the 15th are treated as untimely. Keep your mailing address current with the county so the notice reaches you.

A finished recertification, with the verifications listed on the form, can be mailed to the Department of Social Services at PO Box 3388, Greensboro, NC 27402. Supporting documents can instead go through the county's secure upload portal. If the review packet goes missing, the call center at 336-641-3000 can send a replacement.

Older adults who also need help finding food between benefit dates can use the Greater Guilford Food Finder app, which the county recommends as the starting point for local food resources.

Other Guilford help for older residents

Winter heating help in Guilford has a head start for some seniors. The county says its Low-income Energy Assistance Program (LIEAP) runs December through March, or until funds are exhausted, and that the early application period begins in December for individuals aged 60 or older or a person with a disability receiving services from the NC Division of Adult and Aging Services. Regular applications follow from January through March, and applications go through ePASS.

For needs beyond food and energy, Guilford's adult services programs can be reached at 336-641-3143. They include in-home aide services, adult day care and the Special Assistance In-home program, which pays Medicaid-eligible residents a supplement so they can stay safely in their homes.

Guilford County limits

North Carolina's BBCE listing on USDA's chart, no asset limit and a 200 percent gross line, applies in Greensboro and High Point alike. For FY2027 that line equals $2,660 a month for someone living alone and $3,608 for a couple, as the North Carolina FNS guide explains, along with how deductions move the payment up or down. The maximum for one person is $306 a month.

Frequently asked questions

When is my Guilford County FNS recertification late?

The county treats recertifications received after the 15th of the month they are due as untimely.

Is there a DSS office in High Point?

Yes. NCDHHS lists both a Greensboro office and a High Point office for Guilford County DSS.

Where do I mail a Guilford County FNS recertification?

To the Department of Social Services, PO Box 3388, Greensboro, NC 27402, with the verifications listed on the form. Documents can also be uploaded through the county's secure portal.
