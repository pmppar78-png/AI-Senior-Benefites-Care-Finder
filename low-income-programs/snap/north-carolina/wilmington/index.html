SNAP and FNS Help in Wilmington for Seniors

New Hanover County Social Services, part of the county's Health & Human Services department, runs Food and Nutrition Services for Wilmington. Its page is notably flexible about how an application arrives and clear that proof does not have to come with it.

Office details here come from New Hanover's own FNS page as published on November 15, 2026; the FY2027 limits have applied since October 1, 2026.

Six ways to apply in New Hanover County

The county's FNS page lists ePASS, email, phone, mail, fax and in-person filing. Applications, verifications, reported changes and questions can all be emailed to DSSinfo@nhcgov.com. Phone applications use 910-798-3500, option 4. A printed application can be mailed to, or handed in at, the Social Services building at 1650 Greenfield Street, Wilmington, NC 28401, or faxed to 910-798-7824, and the county will mail an application on request.

Verifications are not required to apply; the caseworker may raise them during the interview. An interview, by phone, video or in person, is required for each application and at least once every 12 months at recertification. The county notes that a temporary waiver that skipped some interviews has ended.

The same 910-798-3500 number, option 5, reaches the county's WIC services, which can matter for a grandparent raising a young grandchild.

Messages from your caseworker

New Hanover encourages households to enhance their ePASS account, which lets them get text messages and secure messages from the caseworker. For a senior who misses phone calls, written messages can be easier to keep track of.

Protecting the EBT card from scammers

New Hanover's FNS page links to the state's guidance on stolen FNS benefits. NCDHHS reports EBT cards being re-pinned through the cardholder portal and then drained, mostly through online shopping, and stolen benefits can no longer be replaced since the federal government ended replacements on December 20, 2024.

Its advice comes down to a few habits: use only the FIS ebtEDGE mobile app, change the PIN regularly to something that is not a simple sequence, and look for anything attached to a store's card reader. If you suspect theft, freeze the card in ebtEDGE, block out-of-state and online purchases, and call the EBT Call Center at 1-888-622-7328 for a free replacement card.

Reading the county's income table

New Hanover's income table, when read on November 15, 2026, was still the FY2026 version, labeled October 1, 2025, through September 30, 2026. Its 200 percent column ($2,610 a month for one person, $3,526 for two) is out of date. For FY2027, which began October 1, 2026, the same 200 percent line is $2,660 for one person and $3,608 for two, twice USDA's FY2027 net limits of $1,330 and $1,804. NCDHHS had not posted its own FY2027 table at that date. Use the FY2027 figures until the county updates its table.

To use the column, compare the household's total monthly income before any deductions with the figure for its size. Take, as an illustration, a Wilmington widow living alone on $2,400 a month from Social Security and a small pension. That is under the FY2027 line of $2,660, so under North Carolina's BBCE policy her savings are not counted. Her benefit still depends on net income after deductions, up to the $306 maximum for one person; the North Carolina FNS guide explains the deductions.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my documents ready to apply in Wilmington?

No. New Hanover County says verifications are not required to apply and may be requested during the interview.

How often will New Hanover County interview me?

At application and at least once every 12 months at recertification, by phone, video or in person.
