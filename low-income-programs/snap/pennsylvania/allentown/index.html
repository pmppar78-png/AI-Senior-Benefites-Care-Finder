SNAP for Seniors in Allentown and Lehigh County

Allentown and the rest of Lehigh County are served by a single County Assistance Office located in the city. Older residents can apply there, by mail, or online, and the office opens at 7:30 a.m.

Office, phone and card-lock details were read on DHS pages on November 15, 2026, and the income figures are the ones in effect since October 1, 2026.

The Lehigh County Assistance Office

The DHS directory lists one Lehigh County Assistance Office, at 555 Union Blvd., Suite 3, Allentown, PA 18109-3389, open from 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. It is the county's office for SNAP and the other DHS benefits handled by County Assistance Offices.

Callers can use the toll-free number, 1-877-223-5956, or the local line, 610-821-6509; the fax is 610-821-6705. The directory gives Lehigh no separate LIHEAP number, so an Allentown senior seeking heating help can apply on COMPASS, bring the paper form to Union Boulevard, or call DHS's statewide LIHEAP hotline at 1-866-857-7095.

Choosing the right application

An Allentown household where everyone is 60 or older or disabled, buys and cooks food together, and has no wages can use the DHS Simple SNAP application, linked from the SNAP for Older Adults page. Anyone else uses the standard form or COMPASS.

For services beyond food, such as meals, transportation or in-home help, Pennsylvania's Department of Aging calls the local Area Agency on Aging the front door for aging services. A DHS contact directory names Lehigh County Aging and Adult Services as the agency for this county: 17 S. Seventh St., Room 230, Allentown; 610-782-3034.

Locking your ACCESS card

Skimming and theft can empty a SNAP balance, and DHS's answer is a lock you control. The steps on the DHS EBT page:

  1. Download the free ConnectEBT app, or go to www.connectebt.com, and create a user account.
  2. Choose the Lock/Unlock Card button on the home screen.
  3. Pick Lock My Card Everywhere to stop all purchases, online and in stores, or Lock My Card Outside of PA to stop store purchases outside Pennsylvania while still allowing online ones.
  4. Click OK.

Unlock before a shopping trip and lock again when done, or turn on Auto Re-Lock. Questions about the card go to the EBT Recipient Hotline, 1-888-328-7366, which answers 24 hours a day.

Lehigh County figures

Because USDA lists Pennsylvania's BBCE policy with no asset limit, an Allentown retiree's savings do not count against them as long as gross income stays under the DHS line. That line is $2,660 a month for a single person and $3,608 for a couple; the Pennsylvania SNAP guide explains how net income then sets the payment, which tops out at $306 for one person.

Frequently asked questions

Is there more than one assistance office in Lehigh County?

The DHS directory lists one Lehigh County Assistance Office, in Allentown.

Can I use the Simple SNAP form if my spouse still works?

No. The Simple form is only for homes where nobody has job earnings and every member is 60 or older or disabled, so a working spouse means using COMPASS or the standard form.

How do I lock my ACCESS card in Allentown?

Through the ConnectEBT app or connectebt.com: create an account, choose Lock/Unlock Card, and pick whether to lock it everywhere or only outside Pennsylvania.
