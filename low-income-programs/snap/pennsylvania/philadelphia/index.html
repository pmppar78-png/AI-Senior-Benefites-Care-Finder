SNAP for Older Philadelphians

Philadelphia's County Assistance Office is not one office but a headquarters plus a network of district offices. Which district handles your SNAP case depends on where you live, and one of those districts is currently closed, so it is worth checking before you travel.

The district list and the Glendale ZIP moves reflect DHS's County Assistance Office directory as it stood on November 15, 2026. Dollar figures took effect October 1, 2026.

Philadelphia's district offices

The DHS County Assistance Office directory lists these Philadelphia districts: Boulevard, Chelten, Delancey, Elmwood, Glendale, Liberty, Long Term and Independent Services, Somerset, South, Unity and West. The directory gives 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. hours for the headquarters and districts. The headquarters is at 801 Market Street, and DHS gives 215-560-7226 as the number for Philadelphians with questions about their benefits.

The Glendale District office is temporarily closed. DHS has moved its households to other districts by ZIP code:

Applying and staying enrolled

Most Philadelphians can skip the district office entirely by applying through COMPASS, which DHS says produces a simplified application as you enter your details. The DHS SNAP for Older Adults page also has the paper Simple application in English and Spanish.

Heating help is handled at its own address in the city: DHS lists a Philadelphia LIHEAP office at 1163 S. Broad Street, open 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., at 215-560-1583. The Pennsylvania SNAP guide explains why a LIHEAP grant can mean more SNAP for a home with someone 60 or older.

Pennsylvania limits in Philadelphia

Savings in a Philadelphia bank account will not count against a SNAP application while gross income stays at or below twice the poverty line. From October 2026, DHS puts that gross line at $2,660 a month for one person and $3,608 for two, and the state guide carries the full table for larger households.

Under those lines there is no asset or net-income test, but the payment is still $306 for one person minus 30 percent of net income. The guide also covers deductions, the ACCESS card and appeals.

Frequently asked questions

The Glendale office is closed. Where do I go?

DHS serves Glendale households by ZIP code: 19138, 19140 and 19144 at Chelten; 19121 at Liberty; 19126 and 19132 at Somerset; and 19120 at Unity.

Where is the LIHEAP office in Philadelphia?

The DHS directory lists it at 1163 S. Broad Street, Philadelphia, PA 19147, open 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., phone 215-560-1583.
