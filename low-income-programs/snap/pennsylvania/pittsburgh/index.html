Pittsburgh Seniors and SNAP in Allegheny County

SNAP in Pittsburgh runs through the Allegheny County Assistance Office, which has its headquarters at Piatt Place downtown and several district offices across the county. Its offices open at 7:30 a.m.

Allegheny office addresses were copied from DHS on November 15, 2026; the income lines and benefit amounts are effective October 1, 2026.

Allegheny County's offices

The DHS directory lists the Allegheny County Assistance Office headquarters at Piatt Place, 301 Fifth Avenue, Suite 470, Pittsburgh, PA 15222 (412-565-2146), plus these districts: Alle-Kiski, Institution-Related Eligibility (IRED), Liberty, Three Rivers, Southeast, Southern and Greater Pittsburgh East. Hours are 7:30 a.m. to 5 p.m.

As its name suggests, the Institution-Related Eligibility District deals with cases connected to institutions. It sits in the same building, Suite 420, at 412-565-5604. If a relative lives in a care facility, ask the county office whether that district handles the case.

Applying from Pittsburgh

COMPASS is the quickest route, and DHS says it generates a simplified application as you type in your information. Paper applications, including the DHS Simple SNAP form for households where everyone is 60+ or disabled with no work earnings, can go to your district office in person or by mail.

Allegheny's LIHEAP office is separate from the SNAP districts: 5947 Penn Avenue, 4th floor, with the entrance at Kirkwood Street and North Highland Avenue, phone 412-562-0330. A senior who pays for heat or cooling apart from rent can show it with a recent utility bill, a landlord statement or a lease, uploaded in COMPASS or the myCOMPASS PA app; the Pennsylvania SNAP guide explains what that heating link does for the SNAP amount.

Income rules in Allegheny County

An Allegheny County retiree with a paid-off car and some savings will not be turned down over those assets if income is low enough. The test is gross income at twice the poverty level, which here means $2,660 a month for a person on their own and $3,608 for two, and a single person can receive up to $306. The state guide has the deductions and appeal rights.

Frequently asked questions

What time does the Allegheny County Assistance Office open?

Its headquarters and district offices open at 7:30 a.m. and close at 5 p.m., according to the DHS directory.

How do I prove I pay heating separately?

DHS accepts a recent utility bill, statement or receipt, a landlord statement, or a lease that lists heating or cooling costs separately, uploaded through COMPASS or the myCOMPASS PA app.
