Paterson Residents 60+: Applying for SNAP

In Paterson, SNAP applications go to the Passaic County Board of Social Services, which has its office in the city. The county's Department of Senior Services, Disabilities and Veterans' Affairs, based in Totowa, is the area agency on aging.

The NJ county agency and county office on aging directories and NJ SNAP pages were read November 15, 2026.

Passaic County Board of Social Services

The state directory lists the Board at 80 Hamilton Street, Paterson, NJ 07505, phone (973) 881-0100, fax (973) 881-3232. Hours are 7:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and doors close to the public at 4:15 p.m., so arrive well before then. The statewide SNAP brochure gives the same main number.

Passaic County senior services

The Division of Aging Services list names the Passaic County Department of Senior Services, Disabilities and Veterans' Affairs at 930 Riverview Drive, Suite 200, Totowa, NJ 07512, phone 973-569-4060, email seniorservices@passaiccountynj.org. Ask there about home-delivered meals and senior nutrition sites.

New Jersey rules that help Paterson seniors

Older applicants have no work requirement, a 24-month certification period and a deduction for medical costs above $35 a month. Approval also makes them automatically eligible for LIHEAP and the Universal Service Fund, which help with energy bills. Applications can be done on MyNJHelps or with a SNAP Navigator.

Benefits arrive on New Jersey's Families First EBT card. The NJ SNAP home page announces a new, more secure version of that card and promotes a lock/unlock feature, which lets a Paterson cardholder switch the card off between shopping trips.

When food cannot wait for the regular decision

New Jersey's application guidance gives the county 30 days in most cases, but urgent households can receive benefits within seven days. One route requires monthly income below $150 and liquid resources below $100; another compares housing costs against income plus available cash and bank balances. Tell the Board about an empty food budget when filing rather than waiting for a routine appointment to ask about faster service.

MyNJHelps accepts supporting documents and displays application status. After approval, follow the renewal date on the county notice. Senior households are generally certified for 24 months, which reduces paperwork but does not mean a future recertification can be ignored. For a card benefit below $95, the state supplement begins in the first full month of eligibility.
