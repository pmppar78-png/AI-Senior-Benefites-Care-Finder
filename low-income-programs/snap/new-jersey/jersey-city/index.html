Jersey City and Hudson County: SNAP After 60

SNAP for Jersey City is run by the Hudson County Department of Family Services, Division of Welfare. Its office takes walk-in applications on weekdays, and the Hudson County Division on Aging is a second stop for meals and benefits counseling.

Hudson County welfare pages, the NJ county directories and NJ SNAP pages were read November 15, 2026.

Hudson County Division of Welfare

The state agency directory puts Hudson County's welfare office at 257 Cornelison Ave., Jersey City, NJ 07302. The listed telephone is (201) 420-3000, paperwork can be faxed to (201) 420-0343, and the posted schedule runs from 8 a.m. to 4:15 p.m. The county's SNAP page adds that applicants can complete an application in person on weekdays and will either be seen the same day or given an interview appointment, depending on the program.

The same page notes that appointments per day are limited, so an early arrival helps. Online applications take about 20 to 45 minutes by the county's estimate.

What Hudson County counts

Hudson County's page says benefits depend on income and expenses, with shelter, child care and child support costs considered and medical expenses considered for elderly and disabled members. It also notes that resources generally do not affect eligibility, although in some cases they may count.

Hudson County Division on Aging

The state list of county offices on aging gives the Hudson County Division on Aging at 830 Bergen Ave., Suite 3B, Jersey City, NJ 07306; phone 201-369-4313. Older residents can ask there about meal programs and help with benefit forms.

Paper forms, verification and recertification

Hudson County says a paper application can be mailed on request: call 201-420-3000 and choose option 1. SNAP-only applicants are given a telephone interview; people seeking other programs at the same time may need an in-person appointment. After the interview, the county supplies a verification list and mails the eligibility decision.

For online filing, follow the state's current MyNJHelps instructions, which include document upload and status checks. The county page still refers to NJOneAPP, an older label. The state describes a 24-month certification for senior applicants, but the actual recertification notice controls when the next forms are due. Ask the Division on Aging for help organizing that packet if paperwork or an online account is a barrier.

Need Help?

Hudson County Welfare at (201) 420-3000 handles applications; the Division on Aging, 201-369-4313, helps with meals and forms. See the New Jersey guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for SNAP in person in Jersey City?

Yes, at 257 Cornelison Ave. on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 4:15 p.m.; you may be seen the same day or given an appointment.

Does Hudson County count medical bills?

Yes, for elderly and disabled members.

Where is the Hudson County Division on Aging?

At 830 Bergen Ave., Suite 3B, Jersey City; phone 201-369-4313.
