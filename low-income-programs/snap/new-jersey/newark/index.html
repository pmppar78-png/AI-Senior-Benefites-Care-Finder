SNAP for Older Newark Residents

Newark residents apply for SNAP through the Essex County Department of Citizen Services, Division of Welfare, the county's social service agency. The Essex County Division of Senior Services, the area agency on aging, adds meal and transportation programs.

The NJ county agency directory, NJ SNAP pages and Essex County senior pages were read November 15, 2026.

Essex County Division of Welfare

The state's County Social Service Agency list places the Division of Welfare at 18 Rector Street, 9th Floor, Newark, NJ 07102. Call the Division of Welfare at (973) 733-3000 (state directory); its fax is (973) 643-3985. Hours are 7:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.; Wednesdays are by appointment through (973) 733-2001, and the office is closed to the public every third Thursday.

The state SNAP brochure also lists 973-395-8000, Essex County's general customer-service line; use the Division of Welfare number for a case question.

Essex County Division of Senior Services

The Division of Senior Services, 465 Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd., Room 102, Newark, is the county's area agency on aging; the state directory lists 973-395-8375. Its services include home-delivered meals, nutrition sites, information and assistance, and health insurance counseling.

The county's food help page lists Essex County Community Transportation (973-737-7200) for supermarket trips, and the Senior Farmers Market Nutrition Program, which gives eligible seniors $20 to $30 in produce vouchers each June-to-September season. Focus offers Hispanic congregate meals in Newark at 973-624-2528, ext. 142.

How Newark residents apply

Apply on MyNJHelps or at Rector Street. An adult child or friend can act as authorized representative, and the interview can be done by phone.

Upload proof and keep the state supplement in view

The state application instructions say online applicants can upload verification and check the submitted application's status in MyNJHelps. They also say to apply even if the household does not yet have every document. For an older Newark resident, benefit letters, the lease and unreimbursed medical receipts can be gathered while the county reviews the application.

The $95 supplement begins with the first full eligible month. An award partway through a month will not necessarily look like a full $95 payment immediately, so compare the start date on the notice before calling about an apparent shortage.

Need Help?

Questions about a pending case go to the Division of Welfare at (973) 733-3000; for meals, call Senior Services, 973-395-8375. The New Jersey guide explains the $95 minimum.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Essex County SNAP office?

The Division of Welfare is at 18 Rector Street, 9th Floor, Newark.

When is the Rector Street office closed?

The state directory says it is closed to the public every third Thursday of the month.

Does the Newark area have a senior farmers market benefit?

Yes. Eligible seniors receive $20 to $30 in produce vouchers per season; call 973-395-8375.
