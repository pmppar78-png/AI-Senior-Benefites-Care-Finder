SNAP in Chesapeake for Older Residents

Chesapeake's Division of Social Services, part of the city's Human Services Department, handles SNAP applications from its office in the Crestwood area. Older residents who received VaCAP benefits are among those affected by the 2026 shift to ESAP.

City of Chesapeake Social Services pages, VDSS SNAP guidance and the SSSEVA meals page were read November 15, 2026.

Chesapeake Social Services

The Division of Social Services is at 100 Outlaw Street, Chesapeake, VA 23320; phone 757-382-2000, fax 757-543-1644. The city directs SNAP applicants to CommonHelp and lists 2-1-1 Virginia for other community resources. Adult abuse or neglect can be reported at 757-382-2008.

For card trouble rather than eligibility, Virginia's round-the-clock EBT customer service line is (866) 281-2448, as published on Richmond's SNAP page; it handles lost, stolen or damaged cards and balance checks. After hours, weekends and holidays, Chesapeake takes abuse or neglect reports at 800-552-7096.

If you had VaCAP

VDSS ended VaCAP on June 1, 2026, and its SNAP page says those cases move to ESAP at the next recertification. Before the change, Transmittal 36 had set the fixed VaCAP amounts for FY2026 at $128 and $198. Under ESAP, the benefit is calculated from income and expenses, so a Chesapeake resident with high rent or medical costs may see a different figure than before. Keep receipts ready for that recertification.

Meals for homebound seniors

Chesapeake is in the service area of Senior Services of Southeastern Virginia, which delivers free meals weekly to approved homebound adults 60 and older. Eligibility questions go to 757-461-9481.

Worked example: a couple just under the line

Two Chesapeake spouses live on $2,500 monthly and rent for $1,350. Their $40 in unreimbursed medical spending crosses the $35 threshold, allowing Virginia's $200 standard.

Renewing through CommonHelp

CommonHelp allows Chesapeake households to check status, upload verification and report changes in contact information, income or household members. A person helping an older relative should keep the renewal request beside the submitted documents so that each requested item is accounted for. The city fax above is another paper route; retain its transmission confirmation.

ESAP may provide a 36-month certification when every eligible member is 60 or older and there are no earnings. Starting work or a change in who lives in the home is still reportable. For winnings, the current reportable amount is $4,750 from a single game; the linked state guide documents why older flyers show lower amounts. Keep the reporting notice attached to the Chesapeake case with the renewal packet.

Need Help?

Local SNAP cases go through Chesapeake Social Services at 757-382-2000, and SSSEVA (757-461-9481) arranges meal delivery. The Virginia guide explains ESAP reporting.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Chesapeake social services office?

At 100 Outlaw Street, Chesapeake, VA 23320; phone 757-382-2000.

What happens to my former VaCAP case?

Expect an ESAP review when the case next comes up for recertification; after that, income and expenses set the amount instead of a fixed figure.

Does SSSEVA serve Chesapeake?

Yes, Chesapeake is in its home-delivered meals service area.
