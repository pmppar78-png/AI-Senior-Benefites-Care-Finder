Richmond SNAP Guide for Adults 60+

The City of Richmond Department of Social Services has moved its downtown operations to 300 E. Franklin Street, and the city has said Marshall Plaza will close. The Span Center, formerly Senior Connections, is the area agency on aging that arranges home-delivered meals.

City of Richmond Social Services and Span Center pages were read November 15, 2026.

Richmond Social Services locations

The city's SNAP page lists two places to go: downtown at 300 E. Franklin Street, Richmond, VA 23219, and the Southside Community Services Center at 4100 Hull Street Road. For SNAP questions, call (804) 646-4110; to apply by phone, call (855) 635-4370; paper applications can be faxed to (804) 646-7018. The customer care center is 3-1-1, or (804) 646-7212 from outside the Richmond area.

The city's social services main page confirms that 300 E. Franklin is officially open and says updates will follow on the closure of Marshall Plaza.

Documents Richmond asks for

The city lists proof of identity, residence, income, resources and shelter expenses, noting that not everything is needed to submit the application but may be required later in the process.

The Span Center: delivery and contact details

The Span Center's Home Delivered Meals program, run with FeedMore, serves homebound adults 60 and older in the City of Richmond and Charles City, Chesterfield, Goochland, Hanover, Henrico, New Kent and Powhatan counties. Meals arrive between 11 a.m. and 1:30 p.m. with a safety check by volunteers, and the service is free, with voluntary donations. There is no income requirement, although income may be assessed. Call (804) 343-3000 and ask for Home Delivered Meals.

The Span Center's contact page gives its address as 1300 Semmes Ave., Richmond, VA 23224, with office hours Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. The main line is 804-343-3000. This is the aging agency's office, distinct from the city's SNAP service locations.

Card problems are separate from eligibility

Richmond publishes the 24-hour Virginia EBT service number, (866) 281-2448, for a lost, stolen or damaged card and balance checks. After identity and address checks, the old card is deactivated and another issued. A changed mailing address must also be reported to Richmond Social Services through 3-1-1 or the assigned worker; replacing a card does not fix an outdated case address.

CommonHelp is the route for case status, uploaded verification and household changes. For an emergency replacement card, the city directs residents to 3-1-1 or a service location. These card tasks are different from disputing how a medical or shelter deduction was calculated, which belongs with the eligibility worker.

Need Help?

Richmond Social Services answers SNAP questions at (804) 646-4110, and the Span Center, (804) 343-3000, handles meals. The Virginia overview sets out the FY2027 income chart.

Frequently asked questions

Is Marshall Plaza still the Richmond social services office?

No. The department now operates from 300 E. Franklin, and the city says the timeline for closing Marshall Plaza is still being finalized.

What is the Richmond SNAP information number?

(804) 646-4110.

Is Senior Connections still the Richmond aging agency?

It now operates as the Span Center; call (804) 343-3000 for home-delivered meals.
