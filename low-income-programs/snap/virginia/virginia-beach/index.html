Virginia Beach: SNAP Help After 60

Virginia Beach Human Services is the city's local agency for SNAP. It takes applications in person at two locations and by phone, and Senior Services of Southeastern Virginia delivers meals to homebound older adults.

Virginia Beach Human Services pages, the SSSEVA meals page and VDSS guidance were read November 15, 2026.

Where to apply

The city's financial assistance page lists in-person service at 3432 Virginia Beach Blvd. and at the Housing Resource Center, 104 N. Witchduck Rd., Suite 316. Applicants can also call (757) 385-4306 to have a paper application mailed and return it to either location, or apply on CommonHelp.

The Human Services administrative line is 757-385-3200, with Virginia Relay at 711.

Verification of expenses

Virginia Beach's SNAP page reminds applicants that shelter, utility, day care and medical expenses must be verified before staff can use them as deductions. Acceptable proof includes a lease, a landlord statement, a utility bill or a medical bill.

One shopping change affects every Virginia Beach household: since November 1, 2026, the VDSS SNAP page says sweetened carbonated drinks are no longer an eligible purchase, though benefit amounts are unchanged. Keep this in mind when building a grocery list around the card.

Home-delivered meals from SSSEVA

Senior Services of Southeastern Virginia provides free home-delivered meals to people 60 and older who are homebound and unable to prepare meals. Meals are delivered weekly, are heart-healthy and diabetes-friendly, and each covers about a third of daily nutritional needs. Call 757-461-9481; the service area includes Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, Norfolk, Portsmouth and Suffolk.

At 70, this Virginia Beach renter receives $1,350, pays $1,000 for the apartment and has $60 in uncovered care expenses each month. Those care costs trigger the state's flat $200 deduction.

CommonHelp after the initial application

The VDSS CommonHelp guide says an account can track benefits, report changes, upload documents and return to an application later. The same tool is useful when Virginia Beach asks for proof of a deduction: a bill showing the cost is more useful than a note giving only the total. Keep a record of the upload and the deadline on the city's request.

For an ESAP household, the three-year certification is an administrative simplification, not an extra benefit. All eligible members must be at least 60 and have no earnings. A change in household size or a member starting work still needs reporting, even between scheduled renewals. The Virginia guide identifies the current $4,750 single-game winnings threshold and cites the federal rule behind it.

Need Help?

Virginia Beach Human Services, (757) 385-4306, handles SNAP questions; SSSEVA, 757-461-9481, handles meal referrals. Virginia's overview explains what changed when VaCAP ended.

Frequently asked questions

Where are the Virginia Beach SNAP offices?

At 3432 Virginia Beach Blvd. and at the Housing Resource Center, 104 N. Witchduck Rd., Suite 316.

Can I get a paper application mailed in Virginia Beach?

Yes, call (757) 385-4306 and ask for one.

Are SSSEVA home-delivered meals free?

Yes, for approved participants; donations are appreciated.
