Dallas SNAP Help for Older Adults

Dallas County has more than one route to hands-on SNAP help. The Community Council of Greater Dallas operates the Dallas Area Agency on Aging and a SNAP Navigator program supported by an HHSC outreach grant.

Dallas aging and navigator pages were opened November 15, 2026.

Dallas HHSC locations have different functions

The state office search for ZIP 75201 lists an HHSC benefits office at 3910 Gaston Ave., Suite 200, Dallas, TX 75246, with telephone 214-827-5210 and fax 214-818-4898. Its program column includes SNAP food benefits, TANF and Medicaid for families and children.

The same search separately lists an office at 1250 W. Mockingbird Ln. for Medicare Savings Programs and Medicaid for elderly people and people with disabilities. Do not assume that a closer Medicaid office accepts the same SNAP work as the Gaston office. Call to confirm the task and access arrangements; the locator did not publish hours.

These are state HHSC offices, not the Dallas Area Agency on Aging or a county charity. Save a copy of documents handed to either kind of helper.

SNAP Navigator support

The Community Council SNAP Navigator page describes one-on-one application assistance, eligibility explanations and nutrition-resource referrals. It says the organization receives HHSC's SNAP Outreach and Application Assistance Grant. Its listed navigator number is 1-800-499-9958.

The program works with 10 subrecipients across 56 counties in North Texas and the Panhandle.

Aging and disability resources

The Dallas Area Agency on Aging is a Community Council program supporting older adults and caregivers across Dallas County. The Dallas County ADRC describes benefits counseling and help with state and federal applications, including SNAP and Medicare Savings Programs. Its referral line is 1-888-743-1202, and the same options counselors help with Extra Help, the federal subsidy for Medicare prescription costs.

Applications still go to HHSC through Your Texas Benefits. See the Texas guide for TSAP and asset rules.

Dallas Area Agency on Aging meals

The Dallas Area Agency on Aging runs congregate meal sites across Dallas County, including locations with accommodations for hearing-impaired people and kosher options. Homebound Dallas County residents 60 and older may qualify for free home-delivered meals. To apply, call 469-839-6700.

A meal program does not reduce SNAP benefits, and a SNAP case does not disqualify anyone from the meals. Many older Dallas residents use both.

Renewal and uploads in Dallas

HHSC renewals are handled through a Your Texas Benefits account or its app, which also accept uploaded proof and change reports. A SNAP Navigator can sit with a resident while they renew, but the account owner should keep the login.

Worked example: a Dallas couple and the 165% line

Two retirees with $2,800 a month in combined Social Security fall under the 165% gross limit for two people, which USDA's FY2027 tables put at $2,976. That opens the Texas BBCE route, with HHSC's $5,000 resource limit. The benefit itself still comes from net income: if deductions bring it to $1,200, Texas subtracts 30%, or $360, from the $562 two-person maximum, for $202 a month.

Frequently asked questions

Who runs the Dallas SNAP Navigator program?

The Community Council of Greater Dallas, which says it receives an HHSC SNAP outreach and application assistance grant.

Can Dallas County ADRC help with Medicare Savings Programs?

Its benefits-assistance page lists Medicare Savings Program help as well as SNAP applications.

How do Dallas seniors apply for home-delivered meals?

The Dallas Area Agency on Aging takes applications at 469-839-6700 for homebound county residents 60 and older.
