San Antonio and Bexar County SNAP Support

The Alamo Area Council of Governments operates two aging agencies. For San Antonio residents, the relevant one is the Bexar Area Agency on Aging, while the Alamo agency serves surrounding counties.

AACOG aging-agency information and HHSC links were reviewed November 15, 2026.

The SNAP office shown for central San Antonio

A ZIP 78205 search in the state's office finder returns an HHSC benefits office at 2534 Castroville Rd., San Antonio, TX 78237, Bexar County. Its listed phone is 210-436-4392 and fax is 210-431-2368. The result names SNAP, TANF cash help and Medicaid for families and children as available programs.

That is the eligibility-office route, while Bexar AAA supplies aging information and support. If a counselor helps assemble a packet, confirm it was delivered to HHSC. The state search did not give opening hours, so telephone first if you need to take paper documents.

Search results depend on the location entered. Residents elsewhere in the San Antonio area can run their own ZIP search and examine the program list rather than choosing a site merely because it has the HHS name. Keep the SNAP notice and any document deadline available when asking an office for directions.

Bexar AAA services

AACOG says Bexar Area Agency on Aging serves Bexar County residents age 60 and older and their family caregivers. Services include counseling on Medicare, Medicaid and Social Security, care coordination, transportation and help with income support.

AACOG lists office hours Monday–Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central Time. Its Aging and Disability Resource Center, Alamo Service Connection, also serves adults with disabilities and helpers, regardless of income.

From counseling to an HHSC application

Apply for SNAP at Your Texas Benefits. A Bexar AAA counselor can help a family member prepare; HHSC sets the eligibility and the benefit amount. HHSC's location page provides office information.

Statewide eligibility details are in the Texas SNAP guide.

Free lunches at San Antonio senior centers

The city's Senior Nutrition Program serves a balanced lunchtime meal to Bexar County residents 60 and older and their spouses. The usual center schedule is 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. on weekdays, and the city says all services are free. Some sites also offer exercise and computer classes or transportation, though not every center has them.

The program is funded by the city and by the state through AACOG. For center locations, call 210-207-7172.

Keeping the case current online

An account on Your Texas Benefits lets a household check benefit status, report changes, renew and upload items HHSC asks for. The mobile app does the same. Uploading a requested bank statement from home avoids a trip to Castroville Road.

Need Help?

Call ahead before taking paper to Castroville Road. The site's AI guide can help you list other Bexar resources.

Frequently asked questions

Which AACOG agency serves San Antonio?

Bexar Area Agency on Aging serves Bexar County; Alamo Area Agency on Aging covers surrounding counties.

Are San Antonio senior center lunches free?

Yes. There is no charge to anyone 60 or older living in Bexar County, or to a spouse, through San Antonio's Senior Nutrition Program. Call 210-207-7172.

Which HHSC office does a downtown San Antonio search return?

A ZIP 78205 search in the state office finder returns 2534 Castroville Rd., phone 210-436-4392.
