Houston Seniors: SNAP and Benefits Counseling

Texas HHSC decides Houston SNAP cases, but the Houston Health Department also helps older adults understand public benefits. Its benefits counseling program is funded through the Harris County Area Agency on Aging.

Houston Health Department and HHSC information was reviewed November 15, 2026.

A Houston HHSC benefits office for SNAP

The official Your Texas Benefits office finder, searched with downtown ZIP code 77002, returns as its benefits office 13838 Buffalo Spdwy, Houston, TX 77045, in Harris County. That office is not downtown, so residents elsewhere should run their own ZIP search. SNAP is among the programs it lists, alongside TANF and family Medicaid. Callers can reach it at 713-433-3145, and documents can be faxed to 713-434-3024.

The same search also returns aging and long-term-care organizations. A listing in that tool does not mean every office handles SNAP: select the benefit-office option and read the program column. In particular, a Medicare Savings Program location is not interchangeable with a SNAP office.

Confirm the service you need before traveling; the search result did not state walk-in hours. Older applicants can instead start online and use the local number to clarify how to deliver paper verification.

Harris County Area Agency on Aging counseling

The Houston Health Department benefits counseling program serves Harris County residents age 60 and over. It can help people understand rights when applying for benefits, receive referrals and resolve disputes. Reach the program at 832-393-4301.

Counseling is provided in person, by telephone or through written correspondence. The department lists client representation in appeals or hearings among the issues it can assist with, and says services funded through the Area Agency on Aging are free.

How Houston residents apply

Apply through Your Texas Benefits or use HHSC's location directory for local offices. Bring the counselor's notes to the HHSC interview.

The Texas SNAP guide explains TSAP and the state's asset rule.

Meals through the Harris County aging network

The Houston Health Department's senior nutrition programs serve adults 60 and older in Houston and Harris County. Home-delivered meals go to people who are homebound, and community dining is offered from 10:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., five days a week, at local centers. Listed sites include Acres Homes Multi-Service Center, 6719 W. Montgomery Rd., and Kashmere Senior Center, 4802 Lockwood Dr.

Meals are arranged through the same benefits-counseling number given above, so one call can cover both.

Worked example: savings, a car and the Texas $5,000 limit

HHSC's Texas Works Handbook makes a household ineligible when countable resources top $5,000, and section A-1230 exempts the first $22,500 of the highest-valued countable vehicle. Picture a 70-year-old with $1,500 a month in Social Security, $4,900 in the bank and a $15,000 car. The income is under the 165% line, $2,195 for one person in FY2027, the savings are under $5,000 and the car is fully exempt, so the resource test is passed. Another $200 in savings would tip the balance.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use Houston Health Department benefits counseling?

The program serves Harris County residents age 60 and over, with some Medicare counseling extended to beneficiaries of any age.

Can the Houston program help with a SNAP hearing?

It lists representation in appeals or hearings among benefits issues it can help with.

Does Texas have a SNAP asset limit for Houston seniors?

Yes. HHSC's handbook sets a $5,000 countable-resource limit, with the first $22,500 of the highest-valued vehicle exempt.
